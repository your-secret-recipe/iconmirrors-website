01 · LED Mirrors

Light that follows the shape of the mirror.

Every LED mirror has a touch sensor and three colours of light. Choose a standard design, or ask us for your own shape and pattern.

One touch sensor

When the light is off, the sensor glows blue. Tap it once to switch the mirror on.

Three lights

Each tap changes the light: warm (3000K), white (6000K), natural white (4000K), then off.

Parallel to the edge

The light band follows the mirror's outline. On a 24 × 18 in mirror: 1 in clear glass, then 1 in of light.
